Things to try

  • Collapse it. Use the button at the top left, the sidebar's right edge, or ⌘B. Icons stay exactly where they are; only the labels leave.
  • In the rail, hover or tab to a row for its name, and click a group (Products, Master) to open its pages beside it.
  • iPad portrait starts as a rail. Expanding floats the sidebar over the page instead of squeezing it.
  • Phone opens it as a drawer with larger rows. Tap outside, press Esc, or pick a page to close.
  • Header and footer open the organisation and account menus in every state.
